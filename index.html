Microstock Studio Pro

A Windows app by Rhizkit. Batch-process AI-generated images for microstock submission — upscale, quality-check, AI-write the metadata, and export ready-to-upload CSVs, all on your own PC.

One pipeline, four steps

  • Upscale — batch-upscale with a bundled, local copy of Real-ESRGAN. No separate install, no cloud service.
  • Quality gate — an automated check flags soft focus, blown highlights, and crushed shadows before you waste a submission slot.
  • AI metadata — generates a title, description, and keyword set for every image using the AI vision provider you configure (OpenAI, Google Gemini, or OpenRouter — your choice, your own API key).
  • Metadata + export — embeds the result into each image and writes Adobe Stock and/or Freepik-formatted CSVs, ready to upload.
Your images stay yours: everything runs locally on your PC except the AI-metadata step, which goes straight from your PC to the provider you personally configure. Nothing passes through a Rhizkit server, ever.

Bring your own API key — no subscription

Pick OpenAI, Gemini, or OpenRouter, paste in your own key, and that's it. One purchase, use it as much as you want — no per-image credit system, no monthly fee on top of what you already pay your AI provider directly.
